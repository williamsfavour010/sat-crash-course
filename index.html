SAT Crash Course: See the Patterns

The SAT is a pattern test wearing a content costume. These three guides distill each section into the patterns that keep recurring, explained the way you would explain them to a friend: the intuition first, then one simple example, then one that pushes you. Once you see a pattern, you cannot unsee it.

Math

22 patterns across algebra, advanced math, data analysis, and geometry. Quadratics in their three costumes, the discriminant as a lie detector, why the mean gets bullied and the median does not.

66 worked examples, with graphs and sketches

Reading

9 patterns, starting with the big one: wrong answers are only ever wrong in four ways. Name the trap, and elimination stops being guesswork.

Tap-to-answer practice with a breakdown of every choice

Writing

11 patterns. The grammar rules on loop (agreement, pronouns, modifiers, punctuation logic) plus the expression-of-ideas questions, which are really logic questions in disguise.

Simple and stretch examples for every pattern

Built for a student scoring around 900 and climbing toward 1400 and beyond. Work through a guide, then get your reps in on Khan Academy and Bluebook. Every missed practice question is just a pattern you have not seen yet.
